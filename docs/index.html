How to play

Follow the egg trails and stomp enemies by landing on them. Flags save your progress on this device.

Golden eggHatches one duckling, worth an extra life.

Shell shieldAbsorbs one hit.

MagnetPulls nearby eggs to you.

FlockUp to five ducklings can follow you.

Move with A / D or the arrow keys. Hold Space to jump higher, Shift to sprint, and press P to pause.

Hold a direction to move and hold Jump to jump higher.
